# Institution logo sources

Logos identify the institutions associated with Yangtian Ye's education and research experience. Original proportions are preserved and all logos have transparent backgrounds.

- `zju.svg` (original white) and `zju-blue.svg` (displayed blue): Zhejiang University official website, [original vector logo](https://www.zju.edu.cn/_upload/tpl/0b/bf/3007/template3007/static/media/mlogo.66388675484ae2a807b2ad65b1d31ca9.svg). The original white paths use the university's official truth-seeking blue, RGB 0, 63, 136, from its [identity guide](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/2023/0323/c74903a2732011/page.htm).
- `zjut.png`: Zhejiang University of Technology official website, [original transparent wordmark](https://www.zjut.edu.cn/_upload/site/00/04/4/logo.png). The white source is displayed in monochrome black with CSS, preserving its transparency and avoiding a background plaque.
- `fourier.svg`: Fourier official website, [original brand icon](https://www.fftai.com/favicon.svg).
- `nus.svg`: Complete National University of Singapore logo from the [English Wikipedia file record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:NationalUniversityofSingapore.svg), [original vector](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/9/9b/NationalUniversityofSingapore.svg). This copy is used because the university's image host requires an interactive verification. The logo retains both the coat of arms and namestyle, as described by [NUS Identity](https://www.nus.edu.sg/identity/elements-of-the-nus-logo).

Institution names were checked against the universities' official English pages. Personal roles, periods, programme participation, research topics, and supervision details were supplied by the site owner.
